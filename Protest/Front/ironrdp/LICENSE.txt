  iron-remote-desktop-rdp.js    @devolutions/iron-remote-desktop-rdp 0.7.0
  iron-remote-desktop-rdp.wasm  @devolutions/iron-remote-desktop-rdp 0.7.0

The package inlines its WebAssembly module as a base64 data URL. It is served here as its own file instead, and
line 4 of iron-remote-desktop-rdp.js loads it by URL: Firefox attributes the module's URL to every WebAssembly stack
frame, and with a multi-megabyte data URL each captured stack copied and hashed the whole string.
